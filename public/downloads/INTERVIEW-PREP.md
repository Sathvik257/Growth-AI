# Interview preparation — explain the decisions

Use these as reasoning prompts, not claims about experience you did not have.

- **Why this asset?** A generic page helps convert traffic but does not create distribution or diagnose a shortfall. The referral and campaign workflow connects counting to the next action.
- **Why these three channels?** Campus ambassadors supply permission and context; referrals extend trust; clubs/placement cells add incremental reach. More channels would dilute seven days of attention.
- **Is 500 guaranteed?** No. The base case is 520 with a small buffer; the coherent downside is 296. Access and conversion must be tested early.
- **What is the weakest assumption?** Securing 20 active ambassadors quickly and getting two genuinely incremental visits per sharer. One visit per sharer cuts the base case to 460.
- **Why recruit 60 plus reserves?** A planning acceptance rate of 20/60 gives a clearer acquisition requirement for ambassadors; ten reserves are a fallback, not already secured people.
- **What does the budget buy?** Capped eligible expenses and logistics, not signup incentives. Trust and useful content drive the distribution. Unused budget stays unspent.
- **Why not pay per referral?** It encourages duplicate or irrelevant signups and makes a vanity count more likely. Eligibility and unique counting matter.
- **What if Day 6 is behind?** Determine whether activation, conversion or reach is the constraint; quantify the remaining unique audience requirement and report a likely miss if access is insufficient.
- **What did AI get wrong?** The original invitation/math mismatch, the unsupported generic advice and the optimistic downside conversion assumption. Explain the corrections in the learning notes.
- **Is this production-ready?** No. It is a working local simulation. Shared storage, verification, access control, retention and abuse testing are next.
- **What did you personally learn?** Answer in your own words after using the demo and understanding the calculations. Do not invent interviews, outreach or outcomes.
